Nanakshahi Calendar Privacy Policy

App: Nanakshahi Calendar 2026 (Android package gurshabad.awaaz)
Developer: PVS Khalsa

Applies to version 26.11.4 (version code 64) and later. In versions 26.11.4 and 26.11.5, owner push notifications were off until you turned them on in the app. From version 26.11.6 they are on by default. Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Nanakshahi Calendar shows the Nanakshahi calendar in Punjabi, Hindi and English. This policy describes what the app does with information. Versions before 26.11.4 were built differently and are not described here.

Calendar content and settings

The app has no accounts and no sign-in for app users. It downloads the calendar and the owner's popup messages from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) over HTTPS and keeps a copy on your phone so the calendar can be viewed later. Like any internet request, this lets Google see your IP address and basic request details under Google's own policies. The app does not send your name, contacts or other personal data with these requests. Your language, appearance, reminder choices and any reminder sound you pick are stored only on your phone. The calendar dates are the owner's published data, not dates calculated by the app.

Owner push notifications

Owner push notifications are separate from daily reminders. In versions 26.11.4 and 26.11.5 they are off until you turn them on in the app. From version 26.11.6 they are on by default and the app has no separate switch for them. When they are active, the app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Installations. Google then processes a Firebase installation identifier (which Google describes as not uniquely identifying a user or physical device), a messaging registration token, the app version and Firebase user-agent information (such as device and Android version details, install source and Firebase SDK versions) to register this installation, manage its notification topic and deliver messages. In version 26.11.6 this starts when the app opens, even if Android notification permission has not been allowed. A notification appears only if Android notification permission is allowed for this app, and you can mute it or turn it off in Android's notification settings for this app at any time. Google states that it keeps Firebase installation IDs until the app developer asks it to delete them, and then removes them from live and backup systems within 180 days. The app does not currently offer a way to request this deletion. In versions where you turned owner push off, the app asked Firebase to unsubscribe and delete the registration token; this is a request and does not promise immediate deletion of everything Google holds. The app does not include Firebase Analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and Firebase Analytics collection is turned off. The registration token is not shown to the owner and is not stored in the app's public calendar database.

When you turn owner push off, the app asks Firebase to unsubscribe and delete the registration token and removes displayed owner notifications from this app. This is a request and does not promise immediate deletion of everything Google holds. A phone without a connection can delay it. The registration token is not shown to the owner and is not stored in the app's public calendar database.

Permissions and why

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: download calendar and popup content and register for owner push (by opt-in in 26.11.4 and 26.11.5, by default from 26.11.6).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show calendar reminders and owner messages.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: set local reminders again after a restart or a clock change.
  • WAKE_LOCK and the Google messaging receive permission: added by the Firebase library to handle push messages.
  • A permission private to this app protects its own internal receivers.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Picking a custom reminder sound uses Android's own picker, not access to all your files.

Links and messages

A link in a popup opens only when you tap its named link. A link in a push notification opens only when you tap the notification; otherwise tapping opens the calendar. Other websites have their own privacy practices. Delivery and withdrawal of messages are best effort, so an offline phone may get a message late, and withdrawing a message cannot undo one you already read.

Owner administration

Only the configured owner can sign in to the admin page, through Firebase Authentication, and the sender checks that identity. The sender keeps notification content and send or withdrawal status for up to 48 hours for history and duplicate protection. It does not list users and does not record which phones read a message.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it in Android settings to remove its settings and its saved calendar copy from your phone. This does not delete the Firebase installation ID that Google holds for owner push. Google keeps it until the developer asks Google to delete it, and the app does not currently offer a way to ask for that. In versions 26.11.4 and 26.11.5, turning owner push off made the app ask Firebase to delete its registration token.
